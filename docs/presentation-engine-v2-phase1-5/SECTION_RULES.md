# Section Rules

## Required

Every deck should include:

- `cover`
- `next_action`

Executive decks should also include:

- `executive_summary`

## Section contract

Each section has:

- `section_id`
- `section_type`
- `section_title`
- `section_goal`
- `section_order`
- `required`
- `minimum_slides`
- `maximum_slides`
- `slide_ids`
- `entry_message`
- `exit_message`
- `transition_type`
- `decision_relevance`

## Appendix rule

Appendix should appear after `next_action` or `closing`.
