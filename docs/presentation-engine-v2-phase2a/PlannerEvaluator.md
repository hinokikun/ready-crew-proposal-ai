# Planner Evaluator

The offline evaluator scores only Deck Planner readiness.

## Score

100-point score with grade:

- A: 90+
- B: 80-89
- C: 70-79
- D: below 70

## Dimensions

- Story
- Audience
- Decision Flow
- Section Quality
- Slide Balance
- Sales Readiness
- Executive Readiness
- Customer-facing Cleanliness

## Non-goals

The evaluator does not score rendered PowerPoint design, final copywriting, Slide Blueprint quality, or sales outcome.
